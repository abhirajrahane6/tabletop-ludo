THE QUICK GUIDE

How to play

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard.
  • Choose one highlighted token to move clockwise.
  • Stars and colored start squares are safe. Other players’ tokens there cannot be captured.
  • Land on an unprotected rival to send them home. A capture earns another roll.
  • Reach the colored home lane, then roll the exact number to finish. First to bring all four tokens home wins.
  • A six, a capture, or bringing a token home gives you another roll. If no token can move, your turn passes (a six still grants its bonus roll).

Friendly rules: no blockades. There is no three-sixes penalty.
